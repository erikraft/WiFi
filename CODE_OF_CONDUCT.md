# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in the ErikrafT Wi-Fi project a respectful and welcoming experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people.
- Respecting differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Taking responsibility, apologizing to those affected by mistakes, and learning from the experience.
- Focusing on what is best for the community and project.
- Communicating technical disagreements respectfully and with evidence.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing another person's private information without explicit permission.
- Threats, intimidation, or deliberate disruption of project spaces.
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional community.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issue reports, and other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct. Maintainers may temporarily or permanently restrict participation for behavior they consider inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Maintainers are expected to communicate reasons for enforcement decisions when appropriate and to handle reports with discretion.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within project spaces and when an individual is officially representing the project in public spaces. Examples of representing the project include using an official project account, posting as an official representative, or acting as a designated representative at an online or offline event.

## Reporting

For conduct that violates these standards, contact the repository maintainer privately through GitHub. Please include a description of the incident, relevant links or records, and any context that can be shared safely. Please do not post sensitive details publicly. Reports will be reviewed as confidentially as reasonably possible.
